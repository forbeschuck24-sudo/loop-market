Loop Market

The marketplace for digital products. List in minutes, get paid automatically, keep 90% of every sale.

1
List your digital product

Presets, templates, music, ebooks, icons — upload your delivery link and set a price.

2
Buyers check out securely

Stripe handles payment. Your download link is delivered to the buyer automatically — you never lift a finger.

3
Get paid automatically

Money lands in your bank via Stripe Connect. Loop Market keeps a 10% platform fee plus a small handling fee per order.

⚡ It earns while you market.Sales, checkout, fee collection, payouts, delivery and receipts all run automatically through Stripe — marketing and earning happen at the same time. Nothing needs to pause.
